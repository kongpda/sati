The CLI refuses words it has no place for (`sati task create Fix the bug` made a task named "Fix"; quote the title), an `update` with no option, and an `activity --since` it cannot read. A write on a task someone else holds now says who is in the way instead of telling you to claim it. `sati undo` prints one line and `sati task show` prints the checklist as numbered steps. A full disk or a read-only folder is no longer reported as a bug in sati. The board shows an error screen with Reload in place of a white page, and its "working now" strip leaves out agents whose lease has ended.
