Plan the roadmap and record decisions in the web UI: "New milestone" and "Edit" on the Roadmap (name, goal, target date, status), and "Record decision" and "Replace" on the Decisions page. A decision is kept as written; Replace records a new one that supersedes it, and the old one shows "replaced by". Long dialogs keep their buttons in view.
