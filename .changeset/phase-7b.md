New `sati changes` (and MCP `changes`): what changed since a day, `7d`, your last session or a finished milestone, as a list, a Markdown changelog built from release notes, or a short digest. `sati task complete --release-note` adds a user-facing line; tasks labelled `internal` stay out of changelogs. The brief shows what others changed since your last session. `sati feature list --format md` prints what the product does today. The board has a Changes page with filters and Copy changelog / Copy feature list.
