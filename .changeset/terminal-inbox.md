`sati inbox` shows in the terminal what the board's My work page shows: questions agents ask you, blocked tasks, comments that mention you, tasks to approve, tasks assigned to you and tasks due this week. `--json` gives the same lists to scripts, `--for` shows another person's. Agents get the same answer from the new read-only MCP tool `inbox`.
